Points: 1 for a personal foul, 3 for a technical, 5 for a flagrant 1, 10 for a flagrant 2, 10 for an ejection. A flagrant is also a personal foul and counts as both. Technicals are conduct technicals only: defensive three seconds, flopping, delay of game and hanging on the rim are the Other T column and score nothing. Technicals are those called in the game: ones the league rescinded afterwards are still counted, so a total can be higher than the official one. An ejection the play-by-play did not record is counted when the player had two technicals, two flagrant 1s or a flagrant 2 in the game. A player traded during the season has one row with his full total and all his teams in order. Top 50 of each season. Technicals, flagrants and ejections are counted from play-by-play, which starts in 1996-97.
