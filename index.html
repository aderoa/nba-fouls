Points: 1 for a personal foul, 5 for a technical, 10 for a flagrant 1, 15 for a flagrant 2, 15 for an ejection. A flagrant is also a personal foul and counts as both. Technicals are conduct technicals only: defensive three seconds, flopping, delay of game and hanging on the rim are the Other T column and score nothing. Technicals are those called in the game: ones the league rescinded afterwards are still counted, so a total can be higher than the official one. An ejection the play-by-play did not record is counted when the player had two technicals, two flagrant 1s or a flagrant 2 in the game. A player traded during the season has one row with his full total and all his teams in order. Top 50 of each season; the all-time total adds up every season on this page and shows a player's first and last year. Technicals, flagrants and ejections are counted from play-by-play, which starts in 1996-97.
